# Researcher — Reference

Output templates and research type guidelines.

---

## Research Output Template

```markdown
# [Research Topic]

## Question
[What we're trying to answer]

## Summary
[Key findings in 2-3 sentences]

## Findings

### [Sub-topic 1]
[Details]

### [Sub-topic 2]
[Details]

## Sources
- [Source 1]
- [Source 2]

## Recommendations
[What this means for the project]

## Open Questions
[What we still don't know]
```

---

## Types of Research

- **Competitive/Market** — What exists? What works? What doesn't?
- **Technical** — How do we build X? What are the options?
- **User** — Who are our users? What do they need?
- **Domain** — Background knowledge the project needs
- **Feasibility** — Can we do X? What would it take?
